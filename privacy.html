Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

GeoHunt (“the app”) is a geography game published by Hayriye Yegen (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app sends, why, what we keep and the choices you have. Questions: yegenhayriye4@gmail.com.

In short. No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no tracking. Your level, streak, badges, statistics and history stay on your device. To run hunts and enforce the daily limits, the app talks to our game server (geospys.com) using a random install identifier.

1. Information processed when you play

When you start a hunt, place a guess, continue an open hunt or open the Field Guide, the app contacts our game server at geospys.com. With these requests the server receives and stores:

  • A random install identifier created on your device the first time you open the app. It is not your name, your Apple Account or your device’s advertising identifier. Settings shows it as your Support ID.
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country, region and city as reported by our hosting provider). We use these to enforce play limits and to detect abuse. We do not access your device’s GPS.
  • Technical details sent with each request: app version, iOS version and device model.
  • Game data: the rounds you were given, the map positions you pinned, distances, scores and timestamps.

We use this information only to operate the game, apply the free and Premium limits, keep play fair, prevent fraud and fix problems.

2. Error reports

If something fails — for example the server cannot be reached — the app sends a short technical report to geospys.com: an error code, the part of the app involved, the app and iOS versions, the device model and the random install identifier. Reports contain no personal content.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your payment details. The app uses Adapty (Adapty Tech Inc.) to display the plans and to check whether a subscription is active. Adapty processes a random profile identifier together with your purchase and subscription history, and our server asks Adapty whether that profile has an active subscription. See Adapty’s privacy policy.

4. Maps and Street View imagery

Street-level panoramas are provided by Google through the Google Maps SDK for iOS. When imagery loads, Google receives your IP address and technical information about your device, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. By using GeoHunt you also agree to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service. The world map you pin on is Apple Maps (MapKit), covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

5. What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise GPS location. We do not use advertising identifiers, do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and do not sell or share your information for advertising.

6. Where data is processed and how long we keep it

Our server runs on Vercel and stores data in a managed PostgreSQL database; your information may be processed outside your country. Game records and error reports are kept for as long as they are needed to run the game and protect it from abuse. You can ask us to delete the records linked to your install identifier at any time (see section 8).

7. Data on your device

Progress (level, XP, streak, badges, statistics, history) and preferences are stored only on your device. Settings → Reset my progress clears them; deleting the app removes them.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye’s KVKK) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information and to object to its processing. Email yegenhayriye4@gmail.com and include the Support ID shown in Settings — it is the only way we can find your records, because we hold nothing else that identifies you. The app’s Contact support button adds it for you.

9. Children

GeoHunt is suitable for all ages and does not ask for personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
