How do I play?

Each hunt has two rounds. Drag the panorama to look around, open the map, drop your pin and tap Lock in guess. You have three minutes per round; the closer your pin, the higher your score (up to 5,000 points a round).

How many hunts can I play?

Free play includes one hunt a day, up to 30 in total. GeoHunt Premium includes up to 20 hunts a day with no lifetime cap. If the game’s monthly capacity is reached, new hunts open again on the date shown in the app.

I bought Premium but it isn’t active

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Go to App Store → Subscriptions on your device, or Settings → Manage subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal. Refund requests go to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A panorama doesn’t load

Check your connection and try again. If Google can no longer show a place, the game automatically replaces it with another one.

Can I delete my data?

Your progress lives on your device: Settings → Reset my progress clears it. To delete the game records our server keeps for your install, email us your Support ID (Settings). See the Privacy Policy.
